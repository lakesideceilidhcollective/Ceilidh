Tradition with a Modern Twist

Bringing people together through live folk music, ceilidh dancing and unforgettable celebrations.

At Lakeside Ceilidh Collective, we believe the best celebrations are the ones where everyone gets involved.

Combining the spirit of traditional ceilidh music with a fresh, contemporary wedding atmosphere, we bring energy, connection and plenty of reasons to get onto the dance floor.
